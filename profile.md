Company Overview
RBC Enterprise Limited is a Nairobi-based technology and AI engineering firm specialising in enterprise software development, artificial intelligence integration, and large-scale digital transformation. The company designs and deploys advanced digital platforms that enable organisations to modernise operations, automate decision-making, and unlock value from data.
RBC Enterprise focuses on enterprise-grade AI automation beyond basic chatbot integrations, building intelligent systems that integrate directly with business processes, databases, and operational infrastructure. Our solutions combine modern software engineering with machine learning and cloud-native architectures to deliver secure, scalable platforms for organisations operating in complex environments.
With a strong foundation in full-stack development, data engineering, and AI system architecture, RBC Enterprise has successfully delivered technology solutions across sectors including telecom, real estate, media, and enterprise services.
The company, initially founded as a bootstrap company back in 2020, has now grown to undertake projects even beyond the Kenyan borders. With 6 years experience in tailored business operations, we boast a fast-paced rollout approach in delivering technology solutions to our customers.
Every solution developed by RBC Enterprise follows an architecture-first approach, ensuring that systems are designed for scalability, performance, and long-term maintainability.
Our mission is to help organisations transition from fragmented legacy systems to intelligent, automated digital ecosystems powered by AI and data.
